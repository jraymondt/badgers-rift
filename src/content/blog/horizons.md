## still no eye deer

![still no eye deer](/img/2026/one-of-the-greats.jpg)

Still no fucking eye deer, is one of the classic “jokes” from my childhood, so it good to see this humour has been applied to a caffeine distribution hub right next to where I have been just offered a job in West Perth. I think, I think that where I took this photo… I’m not going to check it, it’s now part of the Lore!

Artistic license out the deer portal.

Despite what you brain is telling you this image was generated by a fractal program and not a LLM, this is arguably better than anything an LLM has ever spewed out of it’s binary salad hole.

![pink bits](/img/2026/icosahedron.jpg)

That is enough pink for you, time to move onto my shadow.
You go through life thinking you are an ok person, you do your best and you are just average.
You thought you were done being manipulated by the systems in place, you thought you had made peace with the shadows.
Until you convince yourself you can do better, you compress the shadow to fit in to this new mould, and it really just ends up fucking you directly in your precious mouth, like some kind of space marine power-glove.

For the last 18 months the value that remains the most important to me is honesty, but I am not sure I was being honest with myself.
I need to go easy on myself here, it has been a period of great change, mostly all good change. I now return to the nerdy shadows and embrace my shitty art with both paws.

Maybe do some walking again.
